About the Club

Our story, our values, our home.

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars Rugby Club is a proud community rugby club based in Ladysmith, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Founded in 2020, the club was born out of a deep love for the game and an even deeper commitment to the people of Ladysmith. From the very beginning, the Black Jaguars have stood for something bigger than rugby — we stand for community, opportunity, and the belief that this town deserves a club it can be truly proud of.

We are based at Settlers Park in Ladysmith, where our players train Tuesday to Thursday from 17:00. Our home ground is where the Black Jaguars identity was forged — through hard work, sweat, and an unbreakable team spirit that has defined us since day one.

We compete in the Northern KwaZulu-Natal Rugby Union (NKZNRU) leagues, fielding teams in the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Divisions. In just a few short years since our founding, we have established ourselves as a competitive and respected force in the regional rugby landscape — and we are only getting started.

The Black Jaguars are more than a rugby club. We are a movement. We develop players, uplift schools, grow women's rugby, and partner with professional structures like the Cell C Sharks to create real pathways for the talent in our community. Every player who wears the black jersey carries the hopes and pride of Ladysmith on their back.

Community & Development

Building more than a rugby club — building a legacy in Ladysmith.

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars Rugby Club was founded in 2020 with a clear and powerful mission: to use rugby as a vehicle to uplift, unite, and develop the people of Ladysmith, KwaZulu-Natal. From the very beginning, we understood that a rugby club is only as strong as the community it serves. That is why, from day one, we have invested deeply in the people around us — not just in winning matches, but in changing lives.

We visit schools, run coaching clinics, develop young talent, grow women's rugby, and partner with professional structures to give every player in Ladysmith a real pathway in the game. We work with parents, teachers, community leaders, and local government to make rugby accessible to every child in this town — regardless of background, gender, or ability. Rugby is not just what we do. It is who we are. And we are just getting started.

School Development Programme

The School Development Programme is the heartbeat of everything the Ladysmith Black Jaguars stand for. We believe that the future of rugby in Ladysmith — and the future of this club — is built on the school field. Long before a player ever pulls on a senior jersey, they need someone to believe in them, coach them, and show them what is possible. That is exactly what we do.

Our coaches and senior players make regular visits to our partner schools throughout the year. We run structured training sessions tailored to each age group, introduce the fundamentals of the game to learners who have never played before, and work closely with school sports teachers to build sustainable rugby programmes that will outlast any individual. We identify talent early, nurture it carefully, and create a clear pathway from school rugby into the senior club environment.

But our work in schools goes beyond the game itself. We use rugby as a tool to teach life skills — discipline, respect, teamwork, leadership, and resilience. We mentor young people, engage with their families, and show them that sport can open doors that might otherwise remain closed. Every learner who picks up a rugby ball because of the Black Jaguars is a victory for this community.

🏫
Windsor Secondary School

Windsor Secondary is one of our founding school partners and holds a special place in the history of the Black Jaguars. We have been working with Windsor since the early days of the club, building a strong rugby culture from the ground up. Our coaches run weekly sessions with learners from Grade 8 through to Grade 12, focusing on skills development, fitness, and game understanding. Several Windsor players have already made the step up into our senior squads, and we are proud of every one of them. Windsor represents exactly what this programme is about — identifying raw talent in the community and giving it the platform it deserves.

🏫
Ladysmith Secondary School

Ladysmith Secondary School sits at the heart of our community and is a cornerstone of our school development work. The school has embraced rugby with real enthusiasm and the growth we have seen there has been remarkable. We run regular coaching clinics, inter-school preparation sessions, and mentorship programmes at Ladysmith Secondary. The relationship between the club and the school is built on mutual respect and a shared belief that sport changes lives. A number of Ladysmith Secondary players have already progressed into our senior and development squads, and we expect many more to follow in the years ahead.

🏫
Dr Joseph Shabalala Secondary School

Named after one of Ladysmith's most celebrated sons — the legendary musician Dr Joseph Shabalala of Ladysmith Black Mambazo — this school carries the spirit of excellence and community pride in everything it does. It is a perfect fit for the Black Jaguars. We are deeply committed to growing rugby at Dr Joseph Shabalala Secondary and to honouring the legacy of the school's name through the values we bring to our work there. Our programme at this school focuses on introducing rugby to learners who may never have had the opportunity to play, building their confidence, and showing them that they belong on the field just as much as anyone else. The talent at this school is real, and we are here to develop it.

🏫
Harvest High School

Harvest High School is one of the newer additions to our school development programme, but the energy and enthusiasm we have encountered there has been outstanding. From the very first session, learners at Harvest High showed a hunger to learn and a willingness to work hard — exactly the qualities that make great rugby players. We are investing in building a strong foundation at Harvest High, working closely with the school's leadership and sports staff to create a programme that will grow year on year. We are excited about the future at this school and the players who will come through its gates and into the Black Jaguars family.

🦈

Sharks Partnership

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars are proud to work in partnership with the Cell C Sharks — one of South Africa's most iconic and successful professional rugby franchises. This is not just a name-drop. It is a real, working relationship that is already making a difference in the lives of our players and in the development of rugby in Ladysmith.

Through this partnership, our players gain access to elite-level coaching, professional training methodologies, and exposure to the structures and standards of high-performance rugby. Sharks coaches and development staff bring world-class knowledge directly to Ladysmith, raising the bar for everyone involved. Our players get to train alongside and learn from people who operate at the highest level of the game in South Africa.

More than anything, this partnership creates a genuine, credible pathway. A young player in Ladysmith can now dream of playing professional rugby and know that there is a real road to get there — one that runs right through the Black Jaguars. We are committed to making sure that no talent in this town goes unnoticed, undeveloped, or unrewarded. The Sharks partnership is a key part of how we deliver on that promise.

🏉

Girls Rugby

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars are proud to be at the forefront of growing women's and girls' rugby in the Ladysmith area. We believe — without reservation — that rugby is for everyone. Every young woman in this community deserves the same opportunity to play, compete, develop, and lead as any young man. We are not just saying that. We are building it.

Our girls' programme provides a safe, structured, and competitive environment where young women can learn the game from scratch, develop their skills at their own pace, and grow in confidence both on and off the field. We have seen firsthand how rugby transforms young women — how it builds strength, courage, discipline, and a sense of belonging that goes far beyond sport.

The programme is growing fast. Participation is increasing every season, the standard of play is improving, and the culture around girls' rugby at the Black Jaguars is one of genuine inclusion and ambition. We are committed to making girls' rugby a permanent, celebrated, and fully resourced pillar of this club — not an afterthought, but a priority. The future of this programme is bright, and we invite every young woman in Ladysmith to be part of it.

🌱

Growing Rugby in Ladysmith

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars are on a mission that goes beyond winning trophies. We want to transform rugby into the sport of choice for the entire Ladysmith community — and we are working every day to make that happen.

We run holiday rugby clinics that open our doors to any young person who wants to learn the game, regardless of experience or background. We host community events that bring families together around rugby and show people what this sport is really about. We run open training days where anyone can come and see what the Black Jaguars are building. We engage with local businesses, community organisations, and the municipality to secure resources, facilities, and support for the growth of the game.

We also work hard to change perceptions. Rugby has not always been seen as a sport for everyone in communities like ours. We are changing that — one school visit, one coaching session, one new player at a time. Ladysmith is a town full of talent, full of heart, and full of potential. The Black Jaguars exist to unlock that potential and to show the world what this community is capable of. We will not stop until every child in Ladysmith who wants to play rugby has the chance to do so.
