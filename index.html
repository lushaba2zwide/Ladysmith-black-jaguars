About the Club

The Ladysmith Black Jaguars Rugby Club is a proud community rugby club based in Ladysmith, KwaZulu-Natal. Founded with a passion for the game and a commitment to developing local talent, we represent the heart and spirit of Ladysmith on the rugby field.

We compete in the KwaZulu-Natal regional rugby leagues and are dedicated to growing the sport at grassroots level — from youth development to senior competitive rugby.
